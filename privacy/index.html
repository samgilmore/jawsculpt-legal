Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how JawSculpt handles the photos, scan results, settings, and other information used in the app. JawSculpt is intended for informational consumer use and is not a medical service.

Simple version: JawSculpt mainly uses the information needed to run your scans, show your results, and keep your app experience working. That information is primarily kept locally on your device.

1. Information We Use

Photos and scan inputs

JawSculpt may use the front and side photos you capture or upload in order to analyze your jawline, show your score, and generate your in-app results.

Scan results and progress data

The app may also use the resulting score details, measurements, scan history, and other progress information needed to show your results inside JawSculpt.

App settings and preferences

JawSculpt may store the settings, onboarding answers, and basic preferences you provide so the app can remember your setup and tailor your experience.

2. How We Use Information

  • To run scans and generate your in-app jawline results.
  • To show your score, angle breakdowns, and progress-related features.
  • To support your sculpt plan, debloat targets, and other app features.
  • To manage purchases, subscriptions, support requests, and notifications if enabled.

3. Where Information Is Stored

JawSculpt is intended to keep most of your app data locally on your device. This includes the information needed for your scans, results, and general app experience.

4. Notifications and Purchases

If you allow notifications, JawSculpt may send app reminders. If you purchase a subscription or other paid feature, payment-related handling is generally managed through Apple.

5. Third-Party Access

JawSculpt may rely on third-party services where needed to operate app features, process purchases, or provide limited app support functions. We do not describe JawSculpt as a service that sells your personal data.

6. Retention and Deletion

JawSculpt is intended to keep most app data locally on your device for as long as you continue using the app. If you remove scans, clear app data, or delete the app, some stored information may no longer be available. If you need help understanding your deletion options, contact support.

7. Children's Privacy

JawSculpt is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly design the service to collect personal information from children under 13 for its intended use.

8. Your Choices

You control which photos you upload, whether to allow notifications, and whether to continue using paid features. If you need help understanding what data is shown in the app or how to remove scans, contact support.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy over time as JawSculpt changes. When we do, we will update the date shown at the top of this page.
